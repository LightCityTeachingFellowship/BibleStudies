Mark 8&9 Reading

  1. Once it was clear to the disciples that Jesus was the Christ [Mk 8:29], He began to reveal more to them
    1. The same applies to us today—the more we know about God, the more He’ll reveal to us
  2. “Come after me” is “be where I am”. It’s different from “follow me” [Mk 8:34].
    1. “Follow me” is to follow His example—to do as He did on earth.
    2. When we do this, heaven will bring us to where He is, i.e., heaven
    3. When we “follow Him”, we will be with Him where He is.
    4. To come after Him (to be where He is), we must follow Him.
  3. To “lose one’s life” doesn’t necessarily mean to die physically [Mk 8:35]. It could mean to lose dreams or ambitions in pursuit of Christ.
    1. Everyone who will follow Christ must sacrifice something—must lose his life Christ.
    2. When we follow, we must not grumble. Grumbling was one of the offences of Israel in the wilderness [1Co 10:10].
    3. If any is sad or has trouble, he should pray [Jas 5:13]
  4. The Disciples asked Jesus why the Scribes say, “Elijah must come first”, because they had just seen Elijah on the mount of transfiguration [Mk 9:2-10].
