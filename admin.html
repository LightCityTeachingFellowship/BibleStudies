Service countdown

Set service times in Nigeria time (WAT). Each viewer sees their local time first, plus Nigeria time when different.

Open welcome screen ↗OBS screen ↗

Open this section to load the service times.

Weekly services All times in Nigeria

Monthly meetings All times in Nigeria

Schedule meetings such as every third Monday. Choose whether each meeting replaces weekly services on its date. A fifth weekday is skipped in months that do not have one.

Choose the first and last day, including both dates. One meeting is scheduled every day at the default time below, unless you add a different time for a specific date. This replaces weekly and monthly services during the programme; your normal schedule returns automatically after the final meeting’s starting-message period.

Different times for specific dates

Only add dates whose times differ from the default. All other programme dates keep the default time.

This takes priority over the weekly or monthly schedule or temporary programme until the changed service is over. The applicable schedule then resumes.

When the countdown reaches zero

The next scheduled service appears after this period. OBS scene changes remain under your control.

LIGHTCITY STUDIO

My devices

Manage the devices that receive your private test and stream alerts.

Private test notifications — set up my devices

On your phone and computer, enable notifications in the LightCity app, then open Studio in that same app or browser and sign in with your admin account. Select “Register this device” once on each device.

Private tests are hidden from the public and church libraries. Only your registered devices receive their alerts. They open a private video preview in Studio and do not wait for audio processing.
