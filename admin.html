Private test notifications — set up my devices

On your phone and computer, enable notifications in the LightCity app, then open Studio in that same app or browser and sign in with your admin account. Select “Register this device” once on each device.

Private tests are hidden from the public and church libraries. Only your registered devices receive their alerts. They open a private video preview in Studio and do not wait for audio processing.
