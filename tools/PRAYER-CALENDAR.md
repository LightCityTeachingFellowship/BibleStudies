# Calendar and prayer groups

Calendar is at `/Calendar/`; Studio management is `Admin/#prayer`. Public callers see public dated resources and public service times. Only approved, verified church members receive prayer dates, their own assignment, leader names and the current-prayer WhatsApp request. Group rosters and contact numbers are admin-only. All records are nested under the existing server-only `churchStudioPublications` and `churchVideoActivity` collections; no client Firestore access is needed.

The Nigeria-time Sunday anchor is 1 November 2026, owned by MAN. Rotation is MAN → OX → LION → EAGLE. The owner prays Sunday and Tuesday; the next three groups pray Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Monday and Wednesday are excluded. Hours are MAN 20:00, OX 18:00, LION 20:00, EAGLE 16:00. Default group pause includes 6 November 2026; regular prayers resume 7 November. Saturday weekly reminders default to 18:00 Nigeria time. Announcements last 60 minutes by default; they reflect the schedule and do not detect actual WhatsApp call status. All these settings are editable in Studio. A manual pause is indefinite; Send for today clears the pause only for the current Nigeria date and sends that scheduled group one immediate reminder.

Assign approved members, save their optional international WhatsApp numbers, select each group leader, then save prayer settings. Leader and member contact edits are synchronized. Moving a leader to another group removes the old leadership. Revision checks prevent stale configuration overwrites. No memberships are inferred from names or email addresses. Deployment alone does not activate scheduled messages: a saved prayer configuration/assignment must exist first.

The scheduled function runs every minute in Firebase, independently of the operator computer. Daily notices are due 30 minutes before the relevant group prayer, and the Saturday notice targets only the next week's owner. Service reminders target approved church members and can be disabled separately. Pause affects prayer announcements and prayer reminders, not service notices. The scheduler catches up within ten minutes and avoids late reminders. Deterministic notice IDs, transaction leases, per-token receipts and stable notification tags limit duplicate sends on retries; push delivery remains subject to device/browser permissions and OS delivery. Each eligible account gets a durable inbox item as well. Removed/disabled/unverified members and unbound subscriber devices are excluded.

Calendar prefers the recording-title date (same parser as related resources), not upload/replacement time; articles and summaries use catalogue dates. Recordings without a title date fall back to existing publication metadata or a batched YouTube publication-date lookup. Successful dates are persisted in server-only storage; unavailable dates are retried at most daily per running instance. No duration lookup is added. Unknown dates are not invented. Historical dates can be browsed. Current service/prayer rules are shown for current/upcoming dates, not projected into distant past months as invented meeting history. Time-based events are placed on the viewer's local calendar date, and notification links carry the absolute start time so they select the correct local day. Nigeria time is shown second when its offset differs. Publication date labels remain their original date.

Tests: `node tools/test-prayer-calendar.mjs`, `node tools/test-personal-hub.mjs`, `node tools/test-service-countdown.mjs`. Deploy the six functions with `tools/members/deploy-prayer-calendar.ps1` before publishing the website. The browser and function copies of `prayer-calendar-core.mjs` must remain identical (enforced by tests). Phone numbers are used only to prepare a WhatsApp compose link following a member's click; the user sends the message. No WhatsApp bot, call integration or automatic outbound WhatsApp messaging is installed.
